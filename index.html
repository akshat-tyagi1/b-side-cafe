SOME CAFÉS HAVE A PLAYLIST.
WE HAVE OPINIONS.

B-Side started with one slightly unreasonable question: why can't a café care about its records as much as it cares about its coffee?

So we built a small room with two sides. Side A is the café. Side B is the record shop. One record plays at a time, and every record in the crate is there to take home. The coffee is serious. Everything else can wait until the next side.
